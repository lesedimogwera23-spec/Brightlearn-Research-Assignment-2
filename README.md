# Brightlearn-Research-Assignment-2
Researching about diffrent definitions.
reated a Business Analytics research assignment covering 38 essential terms across four areas:

Foundational concepts of analytics
Data, measurement and reporting
Customer and sales KPIs
Marketing and campaign analytics

The project explains key concepts such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics, data warehouses, KPIs, customer acquisition cost, customer lifetime value, conversion rates, ROI, ROAS, A/B testing and marketing attribution. Each term includes a simple explanation, a practical business example and a credible source.
